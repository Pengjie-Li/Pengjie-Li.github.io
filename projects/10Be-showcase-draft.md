## 3. Project Background (Condensed)

10Be is the benchmark nucleus for studying α-cluster structure in light nuclei. The (p,pα) quasi-free scattering reaction provides a direct probe of the α+α+d molecular configuration — a method that had never been applied to this system before our measurement. This experiment filled a critical gap: **the first direct validation of the predicted molecular structure in the 10Be ground state.**

---

## 4. Difficulty & Thresholds

- **Beam-time competition:** RIBF proposals are reviewed by an international committee; acceptance rates are low for exotic-beam experiments
- **Low cross-section:** Quasi-free knockout on a light target requires high beam intensity (400–500 kpps achieved)
- **Novel technique:** (p,pα) with recoil proton detection was newly developed for this measurement; required integrating the SHT with the ESPRI spectrometer
- **Technical integration:** Cryogenic solid-hydrogen target operation + silicon detector array + magnetic spectrometer — multiple subsystems must work together

## 8. Collaboration with Renowned Theorists

### Prof. Didier Beaumel (IJCLab, France)
- **Thesis advisor** and PRL co-author
- Leading expert in nuclear reactions with exotic beams; specializes in cluster quasi-free scattering
- Group at IJCLab (Orsay, France) is a world-recognized center for nuclear structure experiments
- Our collaboration bridges HKU (Hong Kong) and IJCLab (France) — a sustained international research partnership

### Theory Comparison: THSR Cluster Model
Our experimental results were directly compared against predictions from the **THSR (Tohsaki-Hiyama-Yamada-Schuck) cluster wavefunction model** — one of the leading theoretical frameworks for describing α-cluster states in light nuclei. This comparison was essential for validating the molecular interpretation and demonstrates engagement with the forefront of nuclear theory.

### Broader Theory Network
Through the SAMURAI-12 collaboration and IJCLab connection, the project engaged with theorists including groups working on:
- AMD (Antisymmetrized Molecular Dynamics)
- GCM (Generator Coordinate Method) approaches
- Ab initio no-core shell model calculations
